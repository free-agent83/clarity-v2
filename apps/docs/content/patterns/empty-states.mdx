<Gap>Recipes for empty states by surface are not yet written. The principle is below; pattern-by-pattern guidance is coming.</Gap>

Empty states are first-class — never a blank surface and never a shrugging "no results". The system's rule is: surface the active filters, show one clear next action, and keep the visual weight of the empty surface aligned with the populated surface so layout doesn't jump. Concrete recipes per pattern (PLP, search, inbox, dashboard) are coming.
