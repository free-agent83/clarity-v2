Nivoda's voice is warm, restrained, and commercially grounded. Tone should adapt by surface without changing the underlying identity.

## Voice attributes

- **Human:** sounds written by a person, not a policy document.
- **Warm:** approachable and relational, without becoming casual.
- **Precise:** avoids vague claims and ambiguous phrasing.
- **Calm:** avoids panic words in high-stakes flows.
- **Professional:** marketplace-first, no playful filler in transactional UI.

## Tone by context

- **Marketing / onboarding:** warmer and aspirational, still specific.
- **Trading / finance flows:** direct and factual.
- **Error and warning states:** concise, neutral, and recovery-oriented.
- **Confirmation states:** brief and outcome-focused ("Saved", "Filters applied").

Editorial and campaign surfaces can use a broader brand voice, but product UI should remain restrained and action-oriented.

## Do / don't examples

- **Do:** "Memo approved"  
  **Don't:** "Amazing! You're all set 🎉"
- **Do:** "Payment failed. Try another card or contact support."  
  **Don't:** "Oops, something went wrong."
- **Do:** "3 filters active"  
  **Don't:** "You have selected some criteria"

## Review checklist

- Does this sentence help the user decide what to do next?
- Is the language concrete and domain-accurate?
- Would this still feel appropriate in a high-value transaction?
