---
title: Iconography
description: Lucide, outlined, 1.5px stroke. That's the whole rule.
---

- **Icon set** — [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), outlined style only. No filled or solid variants.
- **Stroke** — 1.5px.
- **Sizes** — 16px (small labels), 20px (standard UI), 24px (large UI).
- **Colour** — inherits from text. In a dark context, icon colour matches text colour. Same for light. Never tint icons independently of their text.

## Never

- Filled icons.
- Custom icon designs outside the Lucide set.
- Rounded corners beyond Lucide's defaults.
