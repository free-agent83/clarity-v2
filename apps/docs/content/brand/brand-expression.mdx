Nivoda's brand sits at the intersection of three identities: human and relational (a century-old jewellery trade of real people), luxury marketplace (high-end products deserve high-end presentation), and fintech backbone (a serious credit business underpins the platform). The visual expression carries all three at once.

## The default expression: luxury dark theme

Dark is the default. Soft black backgrounds (`#0c0a09`, never true `#000000`) carry warmth that pure black doesn't. Light text. Restrained colour. Generous whitespace.

## Typography

Serif display typography for marketing headings and hero surfaces. Humanist sans (Inter Variable) for every functional surface — product UI, docs, dashboards. The serif signals craftsmanship; the sans gets out of the way. They don't share a surface.

## Photography

Real people, soft natural light, jewellery in context. No stock-photo gloss. No isolated product cutouts where a lifestyle shot would work harder.

## Violet is precious

Brand violet (`#6330f5`, `violet.600`) is reserved for calls-to-action, focus rings, and moments that genuinely need to lift off the surface. Not a decorative colour. The instinct to "add some violet" is almost always wrong — restraint is the brand. See [Color / OKLCH](/docs/foundations/color) for the full palette and the reserved-accent rule.

## In one sentence

Dark, serif display for marketing, Inter sans for product, warm photography, violet reserved for accent.

The full brand identity lives in the `brand-system` repo (`/visual-identity.md`); this page is the engineering-facing summary.
