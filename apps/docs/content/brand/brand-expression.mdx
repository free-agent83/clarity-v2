Nivoda's brand sits at the intersection of three identities: human and relational (a century-old jewellery trade of real people), luxury marketplace (high-end products deserve high-end presentation), and fintech backbone (a serious credit business underpins the platform). The visual expression has to carry all three at once.

## The default expression: luxury dark theme

Dark is the default. Soft black backgrounds (`#0c0a09`, never true `#000000`) carry warmth that pure black doesn't. Light text. Restrained colour. Generous whitespace. The dark canvas does most of the brand work — the surface itself signals luxury.

## Typography carries the warmth

Serif display typography for headings and marketing surfaces; humanist sans for UI and body. The serif is what tells the user this is a place where craftsmanship matters. The sans is what lets them get work done.

## Photography is warm and human

Real people, soft natural light, jewellery in context. No stock-photo gloss. No isolated product cutouts where a lifestyle shot would work harder. The photography reminds the user there are humans on the other side of the marketplace.

## Violet is precious

Brand violet (`#6330f5`, `violet-600`) is reserved for calls-to-action, focus rings, and moments that genuinely need to lift off the surface. It is not a decorative colour. The instinct to "add some violet" is almost always wrong — restraint is the brand. See [Color / OKLCH](../foundations/color) for the full palette.

## In one sentence

Dark, serif, warm photography, violet-precious — the marketplace looks expensive because it earns the right to.
