Nivoda's brand sits at the intersection of three identities.

## Human, local, relational

A century-old tradition of real people doing business with real people. Independent jewellers, family businesses, trusted suppliers. This is the tradition the marketplace digitises, not replaces.

Manifests in warm photography of real people, friendly human imagery, and an approachable tone. The product should feel like it was built by people who know the trade, not by a generic SaaS company.

## Luxury marketplace

High-end products deserve high-end presentation. Diamonds, fine jewellery, certified stones.

Manifests in serif display typography, restrained use of colour, generous whitespace, and a dark-first theme. Restraint is the brand — the instinct to decorate is almost always wrong.

## Fintech backbone

A serious credit business underpins the platform. Trust, reliability, financial infrastructure.

Manifests in dark theme as default, clean data presentation, and confidence in tone. Nivoda is where money moves — the visual language needs to earn that trust.

## The synthesis: luxury dark theme

The brand expression is **luxury dark theme**. The default experience is dark — soft black backgrounds (`#0c0a09`, never true `#000000`), light text, minimal colour. Violet is precious and reserved. Photography and serif typography carry the warmth.

Everything downstream — colour rules, typography choices, photography direction, even the "violet never on dark" rule — comes out of this synthesis.
