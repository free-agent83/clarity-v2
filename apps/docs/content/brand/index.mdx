Nivoda's brand sits at the intersection of three identities: **human, local, relational** (the century-old jewellery trade of real people doing business with real people), **luxury marketplace** (high-end products deserve high-end presentation), and **fintech backbone** (a serious credit business underpins the platform).

The expression that carries all three is **luxury dark theme**. Dark is the default — soft black backgrounds, light text, restrained colour. Violet is precious and reserved. Photography and serif typography carry the warmth.

These pages document the rules — when a value applies, why it applies, and what never to do with it. The values themselves (palette swatches, size scales, shadow tokens) live in [Foundations](/docs/foundations/color). Brand is the usage layer on top.
