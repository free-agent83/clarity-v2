This page covers two photography modes:

1. **Editorial photography** (people, relationships, trade context)
2. **Promotional banners** (product-only procession imagery)

Use the right mode for the right job. Do not mix the rules.

## Editorial photography

People-first editorial work is governed by three principles: **perspective, light, and palette**.

### 1) Perspective: through the jeweller's eyes

Shoot from inside the partner's world, looking out. The camera position should feel like standing beside the jeweller in a real consultation, not observing from outside.

- Prefer jewellery-trade environments (consultation desk, showroom, workshop-adjacent spaces).
- Keep empathy with the retailer's viewpoint.
- Avoid generic corporate-office framing as default.

### 2) Light: luxury studio, cinematic depth

Aim for boutique warmth and dimensionality:

- soft directional lighting
- shallow depth of field (subject sharp, surrounding planes softer)
- layered reflections/refractions where natural (glass, vitrines, polished surfaces)

This should read as premium editorial, not bright retail floodlighting.

### 3) Palette: moody and golden, with measured colour

Base palette should be warm and restrained: black, white, amber/golden warmth. Colour is selective accent, not constant saturation.

- Avoid cool fluorescent cast and clinical whites.
- Never push to grayscale/fully desaturated output.
- Let occasional product colour moments earn attention.

## Promotional banners (the procession)

Banner imagery is a separate mode: **product only, no people**. Composition should imply scale, consistency, and repeat cadence.

### Banner principles

- **Scale:** receding sequence that suggests depth and ongoing supply.
- **Consistency:** repeated product type and quality.
- **Cadence:** spacing rhythm communicates repeat business.

## Banner specification

| Attribute | Rule |
|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | Wide banner format (~5:1). Never square or portrait. |
| Background | Near-black studio surface. |
| Subject | Product only. No humans, no environmental narrative. |
| Arrangement | Receding sequence; line extends toward blur. |
| Depth of field | Sharp foreground, softening into distance. |
| Colour | Natural product tones; no artificial overlays. |

## AI prompt guidance

For editorial scenes:

```
[scene] + through-the-jeweller perspective, warm directional studio light, cinematic shallow depth of field, premium editorial photography, moody golden palette
```

For promotional banners:

```
[product type] arranged in a receding procession on a near-black studio surface, ultra-wide banner composition, sharp foreground with progressive depth blur, luxury commercial product photography
```

## Avoid

- Bright overhead retail lighting.
- Flat, contextless framing that removes human/trade perspective (for editorial mode).
- Mixing people into procession banners.
- Over-processed, fake-plastic rendering.
