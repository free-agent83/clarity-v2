## Aesthetic

Moody, cinematic, high-end editorial. Warm, diffused lighting with shallow depth of field. Premium atmosphere — think luxury boutique, not bright retail.

### Subject matter

Real people in professional settings. Jewellers, suppliers, buyers. Genuine interaction and trust. Focus on relationships and expertise, not isolation of products.

### Lighting

Warm golden/amber tones (not cool white). Soft, diffused light with subtle bokeh. Professional studio or naturally-lit luxury spaces. Low contrast, sophisticated mood.

### Depth of field

Shallow focus — cinematic. Subject sharp, background softly blurred. Conveys attention and intimacy without coldness.

### Tone

Editorial, aspirational but approachable. High production value. Human and relational, not sterile or overly styled.

## AI image generation

When generating photography via AI (Midjourney, DALL·E, etc.), use this prompt structure:

```
[subject/scene description] + moody lighting, high-end fashion advertising aesthetic, cinematic depth of field, premium editorial style
```

Example: *"Two jewellery merchants examining a diamond under warm light at a luxury counter, moody lighting, high-end fashion advertising aesthetic, cinematic depth of field, premium editorial style."*

## Avoid

- Bright overhead lighting.
- Flat colours.
- Sterile backgrounds.
- Overly polished or fake appearance.
